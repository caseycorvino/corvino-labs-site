Nexus — AI Employees

Not one assistant everybody shares. A team of AI employees with actual roles, sitting in your org chart, doing the work where the work already happens. Available today — for teams ready to run it.

Nexus is the deeper build. It assumes your team already has AI connected to its tools and is running shared skills and rules — if you're not there yet, we'll say so and point you at Foundation first.

Why it exists

A shared assistant stops scaling once your team gets good.

Teams that are early with AI should not start here. But once your whole team has AI wired into everything and is genuinely using it, the next limit shows up fast: one generic assistant holding everyone's context, everyone's tools, and everyone's rules at the same time is worse at every individual job than a focused one would be.

Nexus splits it up the way a company does. Each AI employee has a role, and only the skills, connections, memory, and rules that role needs. Better focus, cheaper to run, and far easier for a manager to reason about.

Dedicated, not shared

They're real employees. They have their own accounts.

A Nexus employee doesn't borrow a human's login. It gets its own — its own email address, its own Slack handle, its own seat in your CRM — provisioned, permissioned, and revocable like anyone else on payroll.

Campaign Analyst · AI employee
analyst@yourcompany.com @campaign-analyst Salesforce seat HubSpot user Drive account Board member

Its own identity in every system — so every action it takes is attributable to it, not to whoever's credentials it borrowed.

This is the part most AI tools skip, and it's the part that makes the difference between a clever assistant and something you can actually put on the org chart.

  • Own accounts — real logins in Gmail, Slack, Salesforce, and everywhere else it works, provisioned by your IT the same way a new hire's are
  • Siloed memory — it remembers its own work and its own context. Nothing leaks between roles, and nothing bloats into one shared brain
  • Its own permissions — scoped to the job. Your finance employee can reach the billing system; your social employee can't
  • Its own rules — what this specific role may never do, set by the manager it reports to
  • Its own skills — the workflows for its job, not a catalog of everything the company does
  • Its own training — onboarded on your systems, your voice, and your process, and retrained as they change

Because every action runs under its own identity, your existing audit logs, access reviews, and offboarding process already work. Turning an AI employee off is the same as turning any employee off.

How Nexus is different

Nexus vs. Grok Bot.

Grok Bot is the closest thing shipping today — persistent AI agents that sign in to your tools and work on their own. It's a real product and it works. Here's where we're deliberately going a different way.

Grok Bot (xAI) Nexus (Corvino Labs)
Identity Signs in to your existing apps and operates them Has its own accounts and seats — own email, own Slack handle, own CRM login, provisioned and revoked like any employee
Memory Per-bot context Siloed per role — nothing leaks between employees, nothing bloats into one shared brain
Shape of it Persistent bots, each on its own cloud machine, coordinating in a group chat Named roles on your org chart, each reporting to a human manager
Who it's built for General knowledge work, any company Your company — roles and rules shaped to how your team actually works
Model Grok only Model- and harness-agnostic, including self-hosted
Cost shape Roughly $120 per bot, per month Uses the flat-rate subscriptions you already pay for, with automatic routing to the cheapest model that can do the job
Tool access Signs in to your apps and operates them like a user Off-the-shelf connectors, plus custom ones we build for your internal and no-API tools
Skills & rules Per-bot instructions; enterprise access and audit controls Shared library, grouped by role — managers set what each one can and can't do
Where the work lands Its own chat surface, with sign-off prompts Your kanban board, Slack, and email — plus a custom UI where it helps
Building internal tools Can write code for you Builds and hosts small internal apps, with permissions, on the same platform

Comparison based on publicly available information as of September 2026. Grok Bot is a trademark of xAI. Both are shipping products; the differences below are design choices, not roadmap promises.

Is your team ready

Nexus is the advanced build. Not everyone should start here.

It's live and we'll build it for you today. But it pays off for teams that have already done the groundwork — and we'd rather tell you that now than six months in.

Nexus is a fit if…

  • Your team already uses AI daily and is hitting its ceiling
  • Your tools are connected and people trust the output
  • You have distinct roles that need distinct skills and rules
  • Somebody internally is accountable for how AI gets used

Start with Foundation if…

  • AI still can't reach most of your systems
  • Only one or two people are getting real value from it
  • Nobody has written down what it's allowed to do
  • You're not sure yet which work is worth handing over
