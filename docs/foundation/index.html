Connections

If your employee can’t use a tool, or see data, how would you expect them to do the job?

AI is no different. We build beyond the APIs and MCPs available, so knowledge and action can be consolidated into a single intelligence layer.

The obvious ones

Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, HubSpot. Ready-made connectors, installed and working the same week.

The ones with an API but no connector

Your billing platform, your scheduling tool, the vertical software your industry runs on. We build the connector against their API.

The ones you built yourself

Internal dashboards and proprietary tools that will never have an off-the-shelf integration. We wrap them so your AI can use them like anything else.

The ones with no API at all

Some vendors just don’t offer one. We drive the browser directly, signed in, clicking through the same screens your team does.

Hosted where you need it

On your infrastructure if your security team requires it. On ours if you’d rather not think about it.

Shared, not per-person

Connections are set up once and handed to the whole team, already authorized. Nobody redoes the setup on their own laptop.

Skills

Document the tribal knowledge.

Once everything is connected, we teach the system how your team works.

Shared skills across the organization create a lasting intelligence layer that doesn’t disappear when teammates come and go. Your foundation layer gets your business. It doesn’t just read reports.

  • Reporting — the monthly numbers pulled, checked, and written up the way you write them up
  • Drafting — proposals, follow-ups, and briefs in your voice, from your real data
  • Billing and reconciliation — the tedious cross-system checks, run every time
  • Research and search — across your own systems, not just the public web
  • Escalation — knowing what to route to a human, and to which one

Rules

Lay the ground rules.

Worried AI is going to go rogue? We won’t let it.

We work with you to make delegation safe. Set rules for everyone, for specific teams, for individual employees. Your managers set them once and they apply everywhere, enforced as restrictions rather than written down in a doc nobody reads.

  • Brand and voice — how it’s allowed to sound, and what it can never claim
  • Spend and approvals — what needs a human signature before it happens
  • Data boundaries — which systems it can read, which it can write to, and who it may contact
  • No-guessing rules — where it must say “I don’t know” instead of filling in a plausible answer
  • Per-role permissions — the sales team’s limits aren’t the finance team’s limits

Rollout

Everybody gets it, or it didn’t work.

It starts with shadowing and it ends with the whole team. A pilot with three enthusiastic volunteers proves nothing. We install the same connections, skills, and rules for every person who needs them, and train the team on what changed.

Week 1

We shadow your team

We sit with your people and watch them work. Which tools they open, which handoffs they wait on, what they rebuild by hand every week. No questionnaires, no workshop.

Then we come back with a short list of what’s worth automating first, and what honestly isn’t.

Weeks 2–4

We wire it up and build the skills

Connections in place, your team’s real workflows encoded, rules set with your managers. You see it working on your own data, not a demo account.

Week 5+

We deploy it and teach your team

Installed for everyone, with training that assumes no prior AI experience. Then we tune it based on what people actually use.

After

You own it

It’s yours to run. We stay on if you want us to, and plenty of teams do. Nothing here depends on us being in the room.

Tell us what your team does all day.

Thirty minutes. If it’s a fit, the next step is a day of shadowing. We’ll know quickly what’s worth building.
